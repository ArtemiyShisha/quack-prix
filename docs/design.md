# Кряк-при — current design

One browser-operated race for up to eight named participants; first finisher hosts the daily meeting. Preserve local roster/attendance, independent uniform identity shuffle, sound, overview, pause, restart and optional WebMCP.

The latest user correction rejects both viscous group collection and recurring global impulses. The course itself must cause close interactions and changes of order, as in marble runs. The open vertical drop and one-dimensional serpentine prototypes let a detached leader avoid further traffic; repeated broad bowls provide opportunities for bodies to meet again while moving.

Current course: four concave circular bowls connected by enclosed downhill tubes, viewed from above. A smooth inward force approximates gravity on the bowl surface; initial tangential momentum and uniform small dissipation produce shrinking orbits. Duck-to-duck, wall, paddle and peg contacts alter the orbit. Bowl two has one slow paddle, bowl three has two passive pegs. The centre drains remain open at all times. There are no random in-race velocity assignments, rank-dependent forces, collecting timers or global shock effects.

The connecting tubes are constrained rails. Segment–circle interpolation determines physical drain arrival time, including simultaneous arrivals within a tick. Immutable arrival time and independent tie ranks preserve order; tube spacing extends into an invisible vertical entry beneath the drain. Bodies in tubes do not collide with surface bodies. Re-entry velocity derives from the prior energy plus the fixed drop, with direction following the tube. The first duck to complete the final tube crosses the visible finish.

Physics uses a fixed 1/60-second timestep. Race progress compares bowl stage, radial approach to the hole, and tube position; this is an ordering aid, not a prediction of the eventual winner. The camera follows the leading bowl or transit and includes the final finish. The full-course view remains available.

Identical bodies receive a fresh independent assignment of names for every race. Physics never sees names or roster identities. Fresh setup and paddle phases vary the outcome; repeated winners remain possible. Roughly 30 seconds is a soft pacing target. Shared emergency rescue at 50 seconds and explicit distance fallback at 60 remain.

Toy water-park appearance: pale tiled floor, curved transparent channels, shaded bowls, dark open drains, subtle contour lines, glossy coloured ducks, upright name pills. Remove event flashes, explosion rings, boost trails and geyser messaging.

Validation covers smooth free motion, actual orbits/contact, always-open drains, downstream geometry, crossing/queue order, collision isolation, fair shuffle, all counts, reproducibility, finite bounded states, duration/freeze, types and build. Statistical checks are evidence about sampled runs, not proof of subjective feel or guaranteed overtakes. Browser interaction/visual QA only when explicitly requested.
